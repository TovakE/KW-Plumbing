WISCONSIN TAX NOTE

Toilet installation versus replacement

Original installation: generally treated as a real-property improvement. The contractor pays tax on materials; the customer contract is generally not taxed.

Replacement or repair: bathroom fixtures are generally treated as personal property for repair purposes, so the customer charge—including labor and materials—is generally taxable.

Wisconsin DOR Publication 207 ↗Informational only; confirm unusual facts with a tax professional.
